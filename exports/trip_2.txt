**Day 1: Arrival in Berlin**  
**Morning Activities:**  
- **Arrival at Berlin Tegel Airport (TXL)**: Land in the morning (flight duration ~1.5 hours from Paris).  
- **Airport Transfer**: Take a taxi or public transport (BVG) to your hotel. A taxi is faster for moderate walking, but the U-Bahn (Line 1 to Warschauer Straße) is cost-effective (~€3.50).  
- **Check-in at Hotel**: Stay near the **Spree River** (e.g., **Hotel Adlon Kempinski** in Mitte or **Berliner Hof Hotel** in Prenzlauer Berg).  

**Afternoon Activities:**  
- **Explore Mitte**: Walk to **Reichstag Building** (free entry, climb the dome for panoramic views) and **Berlin Cathedral**.  
- **Lunch**: Try **Currywurst** at **Curry 30** (near the cathedral) or **Berliner Pfannkuchen** at **Pfannkuchenhaus**.  

**Evening Activities:**  
- **River Walk**: Stroll along the **Spree River** to **Potsdamer Platz** or **Gendarmenmarkt**.  
- **Dinner**: Enjoy **Schnitzel** or **Döner** at **Restaurant Tegel** (near the airport) or **Biergarten am Spreeufer** for a riverside view.  

**Recommended Local Food**:  
- **Currywurst** (pork sausage with spicy sauce)  
- **Berliner Pfannkuchen** (sweet pretzel)  
- **Schnitzel mit Sauerkraut**  

**Transportation Tips**:  
- Use the **Berlin Transportation Card** (€15/day) for unlimited BVG travel.  
- Walk 15–20 minutes to nearby attractions; moderate walking is manageable.

**Day 2: Berlin Highlights**  
**Morning Activities:**  
- **Checkpoint Charlie Museum**: Learn about the Cold War (€12 entry).  
- **East Side Gallery**: Walk the Berlin Wall’s artistic section (free).  

**Afternoon Activities:**  
- **Museum Island** (UNESCO site): Visit **Pergamon Museum** and **Neues Museum** (combined ticket €20).  
- **Lunch**: Try **Kartoffelpuffer** (potato pancake) at **Kartoffelpuffer Berlin**.  

**Evening Activities:**  
- **Kurfürstendamm**: Stroll the famous street for shopping and dinner.  
- **Dinner**: **Sachertorte** at **Sacher Berlin** or **Eis** (gelato) at **Gelato Scoop**.  

**Transportation Tips**:  
- Use the **Berlin Transportation Card** for seamless travel.  
- Walk 20–30 minutes to major attractions; moderate walking is feasible.

**1. Best Hotel Areas to Stay**  
- **Mitte**: Central, near the Spree River, and major landmarks (Reichstag, Berlin Cathedral).  
- **Prenzlauer Berg**: Quaint, trendy neighborhood with a mix of local cafes and shops.  

**2. Why These Areas Are Recommended**  
- **Mitte**: Ideal for moderate walkers due to proximity to key attractions and river views.  
- **Prenzlauer Berg**: Offers a relaxed vibe with easy access to public transport and local eateries.  

**3. Flight Recommendations**  
- **Direct Flight**: Paris-CDG to Berlin-Tegel (TXL) or Berlin-Schönefeld (TXL is closer to the city center).  

**4. Airport Transfer Instructions**  
- **From TXL**: Take the U-Bahn (Line 1) to Warschauer Straße (15 mins, €3.50).  
- **From Schönefeld**: Use the S-Bahn (Line S9) to Hauptbahnhof (30 mins, €5.50).  

**5. Public Transportation Advice**  
- **Berlin Transportation Card**: Valid for 24/48/72 hours; ideal for moderate walkers.  
- **BVG App**: Check real-time schedules and route planning.
