### **Day 1: Varna – A Coastal Cultural Escape**  
**Morning Activities**  
- **Start with the Ancient City of Varna** (UNESCO World Heritage Site): Explore the 6th-century BC Thracian necropolis, including the Golden Horns and tomb of Queen Pero. The site is near the city center, accessible by taxi or shuttle.  
- **Visit the Varna Archaeological Museum** (adjacent to the necropolis): View artifacts from the ancient city, including gold treasures.  

**Afternoon Activities**  
- **Varna Beach**: Relax on the Black Sea shore or stroll along the promenade. Rent a sunbed or take a scenic walk.  
- **Explore the Central Market (Knyazheski Dvor)**: Wander through the historic Old Town, shop for local crafts, and enjoy street food.  

**Evening Activities**  
- **Dinner at a Local Restaurant**: Try **"Varna Restaurant"** for grilled mussels, banitsa, and Bulgarian wine.  
- **Evening Walk**: Stroll along the **Varna Beach promenade** for sunset views.  

**Recommended Local Food**  
- **Banitsa**: A flaky cheese pie.  
- **Tarator**: A creamy yogurt dip with cucumber.  
- **Grilled mussels**: A local favorite with garlic and herbs.  
- **Bulgarian coffee**: Strong and aromatic, often served with a sweet treat.  

**Transportation Tips**  
- **Airport Transfer**: Take a taxi from Varna Airport (VAR) to the city center (~€15–€20). Alternatively, use a shuttle service.  
- **Public Transport**: Buses are limited but available (e.g., Line 1 to the Old Town). For ease, use taxis or ride-sharing apps.  

---

### **Best Hotel Areas to Stay in Varna**  
1. **Old Town (Knyazheski Dvor)**  
   - **Why Recommended**: Central location near the Ancient City and Central Market. Rich history, boutique hotels, and easy access to attractions. Ideal for low walking levels due to short distances.  

2. **Central District (Tsentralno)**  
   - **Why Recommended**: Vibrant area with modern amenities, cafes, and proximity to the beach. Great for travelers seeking convenience and a mix of culture and relaxation.
