### **Complete Food Guide for Varna, Bulgaria**  
Varna, Bulgaria’s coastal capital, blends Mediterranean flavors with traditional Balkan cuisine. Here’s a curated guide to its food scene, including local dishes, restaurants, markets, and pricing.  

---

### **1. Best Local Dishes**  
Varna’s cuisine reflects its Black Sea location and rich history. Try these iconic dishes:  
- **Kavarma** (Bulgarian stew): A hearty mix of lamb, beef, or pork with tomatoes, onions, and peppers, often served with bread.  
- **Tsatsa** (sour soup): A tangy, fermented soup made from sour cabbage, garlic, and vinegar, often with pork or chicken.  
- **Moussaka** (local twist): A layered dish of eggplant, minced meat, and béchamel, influenced by Greek and Turkish flavors.  
- **Banitsa** (cheese pastry): A flaky, layered pastry filled with cheese, often eaten as a snack or breakfast.  
- **Kachamak** (cornmeal dish): A creamy porridge made with cornmeal, cheese, and eggs, typically served with yogurt.  
- **Simit** (sesame bread): A crunchy, ring-shaped bread often eaten with cheese or jam.  
- **Grilled Seafood**: Freshly grilled octopus, shrimp, and mussels are staples at seaside restaurants.  

---

### **2. Affordable Restaurants**  
Budget-friendly spots for authentic flavors:  
- **Kavarna Restaurant** (Old Town): A family-run eatery serving traditional Bulgarian dishes like *kavarma* and *tsatsa*.  
- **Varna Market Restaurant** (near Varna Market): Affordable, casual meals with a focus on local ingredients.  
- **Kavarna Beach Restaurant** (Seaside): Offers grilled seafood and simple dishes at reasonable prices.  
- **Balkan Restaurant** (Central Varna): Known for its *banitsa* and *kachamak*, with a cozy, rustic vibe.  

---

### **3. Premium Restaurants**  
Upscale dining with refined menus:  
- **The Old Fish Market** (Varna Market): A trendy spot with a focus on fresh seafood and Mediterranean-inspired dishes.  
- **Dolna Banya Restaurant** (near the beach): Offers a mix of Bulgarian and international cuisine in a scenic setting.  
- **Varna Restaurant** (Central Varna): A Michelin-starred option with modern takes on traditional dishes.  
- **Café & Restaurant Banya** (Old Town): Combines Bulgarian classics with creative twists in a historic building.  

---

### **4. Food Markets**  
Explore fresh local ingredients at these markets:  
- **Varna Market** (Central Market): A bustling hub for fresh seafood, cheeses, and Bulgarian specialties.  
- **Old Fish Market** (Varna): A historic market offering fresh fish, shellfish, and local produce.  
- **Kavarna Market** (Old Town): A smaller, family-run market with artisanal products and street food.  

---

### **5. Street Food**  
Try these popular street eats:  
- **Grilled Octopus** (Seaside stalls): Served with lemon and garlic.  
- **Shrimp Skewers** (Near the beach): Charred and juicy, often paired with a drink.  
- **Simit & Banitsa** (Street vendors): Freshly baked simit and *banitsa* sold in the Old Town.  
- **Kavarma on the Go** (Mobile vendors): Hearty stews served in small bowls.  

---

### **6. Typical Meal Prices**  
Prices in BGN (Bulgarian Lev) as of 2023:  
- **Affordable**: 5–10 BGN (e.g., *kavarma* or *banitsa*).  
- **Mid-Range**: 15–25 BGN (e.g., a main dish with a drink at a casual restaurant).  
- **Premium**: 30+ BGN (e.g., a multi-course meal at a high-end restaurant).  
- **Street Food**: 2–5 BGN per item (e.g., grilled seafood or simit).  

---

### **Tips for Visitors**  
- **Try local wines**: Pair meals with regional wines like *Mavrud* or *Misket*.  
- **Visit the Old Town**: Explore markets and small eateries for authentic flavors.  
- **Book in advance**: High-end restaurants may require reservations.  

Enjoy your culinary journey in Varna! 🌊🍴
