# Complete Food Guide for Moscow  

## 🍲 Best Local Dishes  
Moscow’s cuisine blends traditional Russian flavors with modern twists. Must-try dishes include:  
- **Borscht** (a hearty beetroot soup with cabbage, potatoes, and meat).  
- **Pelmeni** (dumplings filled with minced meat, often served with sour cream or vinegar).  
- **Blini** (thin pancakes, typically served with caviar, sour cream, or jam).  
- **Shashlik** (grilled skewered meat, often marinated in garlic and herbs).  
- **Kholodets** (meat jelly made from beef or pork, served cold with horseradish).  
- **Medovukha** (a fermented honey drink, often served warm).  
- **Pirozhki** (savory or sweet pastries, like cabbage or meat fillings).  
- **Vareniki** (dumplings filled with potatoes, cheese, or berries).  

---

## 💰 Affordable Restaurants  
Budget-friendly spots where locals and tourists enjoy authentic flavors:  
- **Krasnyi Kholmogory** (chain): Traditional Russian dishes like pelmeni and borscht.  
- **Sushisushi** (chain): Affordable sushi and Japanese-inspired dishes.  
- **Belyash** (chain): Comfort food like pierogi and shashlik.  
- **Kartoshka** (chain): Simple, hearty meals like pierogi and borscht.  
- **Kukushka** (Arbat): A bazaar-style market with stalls offering pierogi, shashlik, and more.  
- **Kosmos Market** (Tverskaya Street): Affordable street food and fresh produce.  
- **Detskaya Sobaka** (Kaspiy Market): A cozy spot for borscht, pelmeni, and pierogi.  

**Price Range**: 300–500 RUB per meal (approx. $4–$6 USD).  

---

## 🥇 Premium Restaurants  
Upscale dining experiences with refined menus:  
- **Maison Lutti** (Tverskaya Street): French-inspired cuisine with a Russian twist.  
- **Koroleva** (Khamovniki District): Fine dining with a focus on Russian classics.  
- **Svoboda** (Khamovniki District): Modern Russian cuisine with creative dishes.  
- **Pirogoff** (Tverskaya Street): Elegant Russian dishes like blini and borscht.  
- **Kremlin Restaurant** (near the Kremlin): Historic venue with a curated menu.  
- **Kaspiy Restaurant** (Kaspiy Market): Trendy spot with fusion dishes and a view of the market.  

**Price Range**: 1,500+ RUB per meal (approx. $20+ USD).  

---

## 🛍️ Food Markets  
Explore fresh ingredients and local specialties at these markets:  
- **Central Market (TsUM)**: A sprawling market with fresh produce, meats, and baked goods.  
- **Kaspiy Market** (Kaspiy Street): Known for its vibrant stalls and street food.  
- **Mozhaiskaya Market** (Tverskaya Street): A hub for fresh seafood, meats, and spices.  
- **Kremlin Market** (near the Kremlin): Offers traditional Russian foods like kholodets and pelmeni.  
- **Kosmos Market** (Tverskaya Street): A mix of fresh produce and street food.  

---

## 🍔 Street Food  
Popular spots for quick, flavorful bites:  
- **Kukushka** (Arbat): A bazaar with stalls selling pierogi, shashlik, and borscht.  
- **Krasnyi Kholmogory** (Food Court): Affordable street-style pelmeni and shashlik.  
- **Kosmos Market** (Tverskaya Street): Street food like grilled meats and pierogi.  
- **Kremlin Market** (near the Kremlin): Try kholodets and traditional snacks.  

**Price Range**: 150–300 RUB per item (approx. $2–$4 USD).  

---

## 💵 Typical Meal Prices  
- **Budget**: 300–500 RUB (e.g., pierogi, borscht, or shashlik).  
- **Mid-Range**: 600–1,000 RUB (e.g., a main course with side dishes).  
- **Premium**: 1,500+ RUB (e.g., a multi-course meal at a fine dining restaurant).  

**Tips**:
