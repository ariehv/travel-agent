### **Day 1: Arrival & City Exploration**  
**Morning Activities:**  
- **Arrive at Madrid-Barajas Airport** (approx. 1 hour from Paris with a direct flight).  
- **Transfer to Hotel:** Take a taxi or shuttle to the **Retiro district** (€10–15, 20–30 minutes). This area is centrally located, affordable, and close to major attractions.  
- **Check into Hotel:** Stay at a budget hotel like **Hotel Ritz Madrid** (€60–€80/night) or **Hostal San Agustín** (€40–€60/night).  

**Afternoon Activities:**  
- **Retiro Park:** Walk slowly through the park (20–30 minutes), enjoy the lake, and relax.  
- **Prado Museum (Free on Sundays):** Explore world-class art (€10–€15 for entry).  

**Evening Activities:**  
- **Plaza Mayor:** Stroll the historic square, shop for souvenirs, and enjoy tapas.  
- **Dinner:** Try **Tortilla Española** (Spanish omelet) and **Croquetas** (breaded potato rolls) at a budget-friendly spot like **La Latina** or **Cervecería El Cubo**.  

**Recommended Local Food:**  
- **Tortilla Española**, **Croquetas**, **Gazpacho** (cold tomato soup).  

**Transportation Tips:**  
- Use **Madrid Metro** (€1.50 per ride) for short distances. Purchase a **T-Casual 72-hour pass** (€12) for unlimited travel.  
- Avoid walking long distances; use taxis for transfers.  

---

### **Day 2: Cultural & Historical Exploration**  
**Morning Activities:**  
- **Royal Palace of Madrid:** Explore the opulent palace (€15–€20).  
- **Walk to Plaza Mayor:** 15-minute stroll (avoid heavy bags).  

**Afternoon Activities:**  
- **Mercado de San Miguel:** Sample local snacks like **empanadas** and **churros**.  
- **Puerta del Sol:** Visit the iconic square and the **Clock Tower**.  

**Evening Activities:**  
- **Dinner:** Try **Cocido Madrileño** (a hearty stew) at **Restaurante El Corte Inglés** or **Casa Botín** (reserve in advance).  
- **Nightlife:** Enjoy live flamenco in a budget-friendly venue like **Café de La Luz**.  

**Recommended Local Food:**  
- **Cocido Madrileño**, **Empanadas**, **Churros con Chocolate**.  

**Transportation Tips:**  
- Use the **Madrid Metro** for most routes. The **T-Cas,ual pass** is cost-effective.  
- Avoid walking in dark alleys; stick to well-lit areas.  

---

### **Day 3: Day Trip to Toledo**  
**Morning Activities:**  
- **Train to Toledo:** Take the **Renfe Alvia** train from **Madrid-Atocha Station** (€12–€15, 1 hour).  
- **Explore Toledo:** Walk slowly through the medieval city (1–2 hours). Highlights: **Toledo Cathedral**, **Alcázar**, and **Museum of Religious Arts**.  

**Afternoon Activities:**  
- **Dinner in Toledo:** Try **Perrito** (a local favorite) for **Cordero Asado** (roast lamb).  
- **Return to Madrid:** Take the same train back (€12–€15).  

**Evening Activities:**  
- **Return to Hotel:** Check out and relax.  
- **Optional:** Visit **El Retiro Park** one last time for sunset.  

**Recommended Local Food:**  
- **Cordero Asado**, **Tarta de Santiago** (sweet bread), **Pan de Vino** (wine bread).  

**Transportation Tips:**  
- Book train tickets in advance via **Renfe** or **Omio**.  
- Use a taxi from Toledo Station to your hotel if needed.

### **3. Airport Transfer Instructions**  
- **From Madrid-Barajas to Retiro:**  
  - **Taxi:** €10–15 (20–30 minutes).  
  - **Shuttle:** €8–12 (25–40 minutes).  
  - **Metro:** Take Line 8 to **Retiro Station** (€1.50, 30 minutes).
